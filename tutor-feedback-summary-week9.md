## Top 3 Actions We're Taking

Based on your feedback, here's what we're doing right now:

### Action 1: Adding Game-Based Elements
We are introducing game-like practice rounds with points, timers, and rewards in the practice sections — starting with Grade 1–3 chapters. Think Quizizz-style quick rounds at the end of lessons. Several of you asked for this, and we agree it will boost engagement.

### Action 2: Replacing Drag-and-Drop with Click/Type for Grade 1
For Grade 1 lessons, we are replacing drag-and-drop interactions with click-to-select and type-in responses. We heard clearly that drag-and-drop is a barrier for young learners. This change starts with "Magic with More Numbers" and "Together and Apart" chapters.

### Action 3: Fixing Technical Issues
We are logging and fixing every bug you reported — simulation sync issues, incorrect answer validation, and selection color visibility. If you flagged a specific question (like Q1a in Practice not accepting "2"), it's being tracked and fixed.
